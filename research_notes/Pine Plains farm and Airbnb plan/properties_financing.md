## Q2. Financing: one loan for house + farm + STR?

### Takeaway
No standard product cleanly covers all three. Conventional (Fannie/Freddie) loans exclude agricultural-type properties and limit rental use on second homes. DSCR/STR investor loans typically cap acreage at about 2–20 acres and often exclude rural properties. The most realistic single-loan route is a Farm Credit "Country Living" or farm real estate loan, which appraises every acre, has no acreage cap and can cover multiple parcels. Its occupancy and STR rules must be confirmed directly. FSA loans require the buyer to operate the farm. Rates are about 7.1% for a 30-year fixed (PMMS, mid-November 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Farm Credit East (Country Living / rural home loans):**
- Country Living is aimed at properties with "farm type features, extra acreage and/or multiple pieces of real estate collateral." A house must be part of the collateral, and the 2025 blog describes no acreage cap. — [Farm Credit East blog, 2025-04-15](https://www.farmcrediteast.com/resources/todays-harvest-Blog/250415CountryLivingLoansOffersMoreThanFarmFinancing)
- "Country Living from Farm Credit East can finance any sized lot or parcel and we appraise every acre so you can utilize the full value of your real estate." — [Farm Credit East, 2019](https://farmcrediteast.com/en/resources/todays-harvest-Blog/a-guide-to-financing-rural-property)
- It can finance multiple parcels under one mortgage, or use equity through a refinance or second-position loan. — [Farm Credit East, 2023-12-12](https://www.farmcrediteast.com/resources/todays-harvest-Blog/231212YouWantToBuyMultipleParcels)
- Rural home loans page: [farmcrediteast.com/FINANCING/Rural-Home-Loans](https://www.farmcrediteast.com/FINANCING/Rural-Home-Loans)

**Fannie Mae conventional:**
- Second home rules: the borrower must occupy the home for some part of the year; it must be a one-unit dwelling under the borrower's exclusive control; it "cannot be primarily used or operated as a rental property"; it cannot be under a management agreement that gives a firm control over occupancy; and rental income cannot be used to qualify. — [Fannie Mae B2-1.1-01 Occupancy Types](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b2-1.1-01/occupancy-types)
- Fannie Mae does not purchase loans secured by agricultural-type properties such as farms or ranches. Sites larger than typical for the area require lender review, and the property must represent the highest and best use. — [Fannie Mae B4-1.3-04 Site Section](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-1.3-04/site-section-appraisal-report); [B2-3-01 General Property Eligibility](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b2-3-01/general-property-eligibility)
- New STR income topic B3-3.8-03 (SEL-2026-08, 2026-09-02) covers short-term rental income on one-unit subject properties. The lender must document a current housing payment before using subject-property rent. Secondary summaries report a 50% reduction applied to gross STR income, comparables or MLS/property-manager STR data, and a requirement that the STR be legally permitted and locally registered. I did not see the full text. — [Fannie Mae B3-3.8-03](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-3.8-03/rental-income-subject-property-short-term-rental); [National Mortgage Professional](https://nationalmortgageprofessional.com/news/fannie-changes-how-rent-former-home-counts); [Newrez bulletin 2026-071](https://www.newrezcorrespondent.com/bulletins/announcement-2026-071-fannie-mae-selling-guide-sel-2026-08/)

**DSCR / STR investor loans:**
- There is no single standard. DSCR acreage maximums commonly run 2–20 acres, and 5 or 10 acres is common. — [BiggerPockets forum](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/517/topics/1294455-what-is-the-maximum-acreage-allowed-for-a-dscr-loan)
- One rural DSCR program caps lot size at under 10 acres. Another guide lists a 65% max LTV and restrictions on STR income for rural purchases. — [OfferMarket rural DSCR](https://www.offermarket.us/blog/rural-dscr-loan); [OfferMarket requirements](https://www.offermarket.us/blog/dscr-loan-requirements)
- Some lenders list rural properties as ineligible for DSCR. — [Visio Lending](https://visiolending.com/dscr)

**USDA FSA:**
- The Down Payment loan covers up to 45% of the lesser of purchase price, appraised value or $667,000, a maximum of about $300,150. The buyer needs at least 5% cash down. It is limited to beginning, socially disadvantaged or veteran farmers who meet general Farm Ownership requirements. The rate was 1.750% in April 2026, and direct FO was 5.75% in April 2026. — [FSA Beginning Farmers fact sheet (2026-01)](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2026-01/FSA-Loan%20Beginning%20Farmers%20and%20Ranchers.pdf); [FSA farm loan chart](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2025-11/FSA_farm_loan_info_chart%20%282%29.pdf); [7 CFR 764.203 mirror](https://www.customsmobile.com/regulations/expand/title7_chapterVII_part764_subpartE_section764.203)
- Beginning farmer means under 10 years operating a farm, and the applicant must not own more than 30% of the average county farm size. Direct FO also requires an eligible farm enterprise and farm-management experience. The direct FO limit is $600,000. A proposed Agricultural Act of 2026 would raise it to $850,000; enactment is not confirmed. — [Ambrook summary](https://ambrook.com/funding/farm-ownership-down-payment-loan); [Farm Bureau](https://www.fb.org/intel/markets/financing-the-farm-a-look-at-usda-farm-loan-programs); [FSA Farm Loan Overview](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2025-11/FSA-Farm%20Loan%20Overview_Fact%20Sheet.pdf)

**Rates (November 2026):**
- Freddie Mac PMMS 30-year fixed averaged 7.18% on 2026-11-05 (down from 7.24%; 6.22% a year earlier). — [Freddie Mac PMMS](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)
- 2026-11-12: 30-year at 7.11% and 15-year at 6.45%, from secondary reporting I could not confirm on Freddie's own page. — [Norada Real Estate](https://www.noradarealestate.com/blog/mortgage-rates-drop-declines-stretch-seven-week-low/); [RISMedia 2026-11-05](https://www.rismedia.com/2026/11/05/mortgage-rates-edge-down-third-straight-week/)

### Inferences
- **Best single-loan path:** Farm Credit East Country Living or a farm real estate loan. It accepts multi-parcel, any-acreage collateral with barns, which conventional lenders won't. STR income would likely be treated as supplemental business income, not qualifying rent, but this must be confirmed with the lender.
- **Conventional second-home loan:** this only works if the property is not "primarily" a rental, the farm is a hobby rather than income-producing, and the acreage is typical for the area. That conflicts with a full-time Airbnb plus working farm.
- **Investment-property conventional or DSCR:** acreage caps (often 10 ac or less) and agricultural exclusions rule most 10–80 acre farms out.
- **FSA DPL/FO:** possible if the buyer will actually operate the farm and has the experience. FSA can be paired with a commercial or Farm Credit lender (FSA covers 45%, the buyer puts 5% down, the other lender covers about 50%). The house can be part of a farm purchase, but an STR-centric plan may not count as an "eligible farm enterprise."
- **Split-financing option:** subdivide or carve out the house on a small lot with a conventional or DSCR loan, and finance the remaining acreage and barns with a Farm Credit or FSA land loan.

- The full B3-3.8-03 text (the 50% factor and eligibility) was not read in full.
- I did not retrieve current Farm Credit East rate quotes or FSA rates for November 2026.

## Q3. Does the owner have to live on site, for financing or for the farm?

### Takeaway
It depends on the loan. Conventional primary-residence loans require occupancy, and second-home loans require personal use for part of the year and exclusive control (no management company controlling occupancy). DSCR loans require no occupancy but limit acreage. FSA requires the borrower to operate the farm, not necessarily live there. If the buyer lives elsewhere, the realistic options are investor/DSCR (acreage-limited), Farm Credit (terms to confirm) or cash/seller financing.

### Cited Findings
- Second home: the borrower must occupy it for some portion of the year, it must be under the borrower's exclusive control, it must not be primarily a rental, and it must not be under a management agreement controlling occupancy. — [Fannie Mae B2-1.1-01](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b2-1.1-01/occupancy-types)
- Using subject-property STR income to qualify requires documenting a current housing payment, which implies the borrower lives elsewhere. — [Fannie Mae B3-3.8-03](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-3.8-03/rental-income-subject-property-short-term-rental)
- FSA FO/DPL eligibility requires an eligible farm enterprise and farm-management experience. It is aimed at operators, not passive owners. — [FSA Farm Loan Overview](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2025-11/FSA-Farm%20Loan%20Overview_Fact%20Sheet.pdf); [Ambrook](https://ambrook.com/funding/farm-ownership-down-payment-loan)
- DSCR loans are business-purpose loans with no owner-occupancy requirement but acreage limits. — [Griffin Funding business purpose loans](https://griffinfunding.com/commercial-loans/business-purpose-loans/); [BiggerPockets](https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/517/topics/1294455-what-is-the-maximum-acreage-allowed-for-a-dscr-loan)

### Inferences
- If the buyer lives elsewhere and runs a full-time Airbnb, the main house can't be financed as a second home. It would be an investment property, and Fannie Mae's agricultural exclusion still applies.
- If the buyer doesn't live on site, the farm will likely need a resident manager or tenant farmer. A guest cottage or second dwelling helps here, either for a caretaker or as the STR unit while the main house is used part-time.
- New York agricultural assessment and local STR permit rules also matter for an absentee owner. Those are outside this research scope and should be checked with Pine Plains/Milan town code.

### Gaps
- I found no source on Farm Credit East's requirements for non-owner-occupied rural homes.
- Local (Pine Plains/Milan/Ancram) STR permit and registration requirements were not researched here.
