# Short-Term Rental (Airbnb / VRBO / Hipcamp Farm Stay) Rules and Market: Farmhouse on a Working Farm, Pine Plains NY and Neighbors

Research date: 2026-11-17. Already known and not repeated: AirROI Nov 2025 to Oct 2026 Pine Plains (27 listings, ~$51K/yr average, $483 ADR, 39% occupancy), and no Pine Plains STR law as of June 2026.

## 1. Pine Plains and neighboring-town STR regulation status (as of Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
I found no evidence that Pine Plains has adopted an STR law as of Nov 2026. The town is still in a discussion phase that started in 2024. The town attorney circulated two model laws, from the Rhinebeck and Pleasant Valley towns, in Nov 2025. Pine Plains' only 2026 action that touches STRs is an ADU tax-exemption law that excludes STR use. Among the neighbors, Stanford and Ancram already require permits, Milan has a 2022 law and was tightening it in 2026, and Amenia and North East appear unregulated, though that is less certain.

### Cited Findings
**Pine Plains**
- Town Board minutes, Nov 20, 2025: "the town attorney discussed potential short term rental law and will send over 2 examples, one from the Town of Rhinebeck and one from the Town of Pleasant Valley." — [Pine Plains Town Board minutes 2025-11-20](https://www.pineplains-ny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2025-11-20-Town-of-Pine-Plains-Town-Board-Meeting.pdf)
- A board member circulated example ordinances and offered an attorney-client session to review "hosted and non-hosted models" and enforcement options before the board considers a local law. This is an AI-generated meeting summary, a secondary source. — [CitizenPortal](https://citizenportal.ai/articles/10694378/new-york/dutchess-county/pine-plains/board-discusses-options-for-short-term-rental-rules-attorney-review-offered)
- My searches of Herald coverage, the town site and general web for "Pine Plains short-term rental local law / public hearing" found no STR public hearing notice and no adopted STR law through Nov 2026. The town's 2026 hearing notices I found cover an ADU law and water rates. — [ADU hearing notice, Feb 2026](https://www.pineplains-ny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Notice-of-Public-Hearing-ADU-Law-002.pdf); [Herald, water-rate hearing](https://www.newpineplainsherald.org/pine-plains-schedules-town-hall-design-forum-and-hearing-on-water-rate-increase/)
- The 2026 draft Pine Plains local law (PDF created Feb 6, 2026) rescinds Local Law No. 3 of 2025 and adopts the RPTL §421-p ADU tax exemption. The exemption is 100% in year 1, stepping down over 10 years. It applies to accessory, ECHO and guest/caretaker cottages only if they are used for long-term housing (leases of one year or more): "Short term rentals (STRs) such as Air B&Bs or similar vacation stays are not eligible for this exemption." — [Pine Plains draft Local Law 2026](https://www.pineplains-ny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Local-Law-Rescind-of-Local-Law-No.-3-of-2025-002.pdf)
- Background, from Mar 2024 (page updated Jan 11, 2026):
  - Pine Plains had 22 STR units in 2019 and 32 by Q3 2023. That is 2.6% of 1,175 housing units, about 3 times the Dutchess average of 0.8%.
  - 87% of Dutchess STRs are entire homes.
  - The Zoning Review Committee did not object to owner-occupied STRs or to owners renting while they are away. It did resist investor conversions ("We want to discourage the purchase of investor units").
  - Any rules would likely apply only to new conversions.
  - The ideas floated were a primary-residency requirement, an annual permit fee, septic updates, and caps of the kind used in Poughkeepsie (per owner) and Kingston (1% of housing stock).
  - The county offers to pay for Granicus monitoring ($2,500/yr for fewer than 50 listings).

  — [New Pine Plains Herald, "Pine Plains Opens the Door to STR Regulations"](https://www.newpineplainsherald.org/pine-plains-opens-the-door-to-short-term-rental-regulations/)
- A 2025 regional guide lists Pine Plains as "Unregulated (Pending)." — [Fresh Air NY 2025 guide](https://freshairny.com/blog/2025-guide-hudson-valley-catskills-str-regulations)

**Milan**
- Milan regulates STRs under Local Law No. 2 of 2022, which defines an STR as a stay of fewer than 30 days. On Apr 20, 2026 the board set a hearing on amendments:
  - compliance with state fire and building code
  - smoke and CO detectors, extinguishers and GFCI outlets
  - two safe exits per bedroom (a window can count)
  - an annual inspection by the zoning enforcement officer
  - emergency contacts, parking rules and pet policy given to guests

  — [Herald, Apr 23, 2026](https://www.newpineplainsherald.org/milan-sets-hearing-on-short-term-rental-safety-law/)
- On May 18, 2026 the hearing was held open, with more comment set for June 15 before a vote. A local silo-home Airbnb host said the upgrades "would be so cost prohibitive that I may no longer be able to do it." — [Herald, May 21, 2026](https://www.newpineplainsherald.org/milan-weighs-tighter-rules-for-short-term-rentals/)
- I could not confirm whether Milan adopted the amendment after June 15, 2026 (see Gaps).

**Stanford**
- An STR permit is required for any rental of 30 consecutive days or fewer (Town Code §164-19.6). Terms from the permit application:
  - $500 fee covering two years
  - satisfactory Building Inspector inspection before issuance and at each renewal
  - permits are non-transferable
  - floor plan required
  - 2 off-street parking spaces plus 1 per rented bedroom
  - septic and well diagram
  - proof of property insurance and a liability certificate "indicating the premises is rated as a short-term rental"
  - a local contact or managing agent
  - **STRs are not allowed in ADUs**

  — [Stanford STR permit application, uploaded Sept 2025](https://stanfordny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Short-Term-Rental-Permit-Application-1.pdf)
- The STR law was introduced and amended in 2025, as Local Law 4 and then Local Law 5. — [Resolution 3A-2025](https://stanfordny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Town-of-Stanford-Resolution-3A-of-2025-Introduce-Local-Law-4-Short-Term-Rental-Accomodations.pdf); [Resolution July 10, 2025](https://stanfordny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Resolution-to-Introduce-LL-for-STR-7-10-25.pdf)

**Ancram (Columbia County)**
- A 2020 local law requires a Planning Board special-use permit for all STRs, plus a building-permit inspection. — [Millerton News](https://millertonnews.com/millerton-news-regional/ancram-town-board-passes-local-law-regulate-short-term-rentals)
- The application lists a $250 operating permit fee and occupancy limits tied to bedrooms. This comes from a search snippet; the form is dated about Jan 2024. — [Ancram STR application](https://www.ancramny.org/ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Short-Term-Rental-Application.pdf)

**Amenia**
- The Housing Board decided to leave STRs unregulated pending Comprehensive Plan review. The article is undated in my snippet and appears to be a few years old (Sept 19 meeting). — [Millerton News](https://millertonnews.com/amenia-board-weighs-in-on-airbnbs-and-short-term-rentals)

**North East / Village of Millerton**
- I found no STR law. The only hit was a third-party directory. — (no reliable source)

### Inferences
- Right now the Pine Plains farmhouse can likely operate without a town STR permit. However, zoning (Town Code Ch. 275) and building and fire code still apply, and so does septic capacity.
- If Pine Plains adopts the Rhinebeck or Pleasant Valley model, expect the same elements that surround it: a permit with a fee, an inspection, a parking and occupancy cap, a local contact, and possibly a hosted vs. non-hosted distinction or a primary-residence test.
- The committee signaled that existing operators may be grandfathered, so a farm that establishes a documented STR history before any law passes may be better placed. That is speculative.
- A farmhouse where the farm owner or operator lives on the parcel might qualify as "hosted" under such models.
- Do not plan to put the STR in a new ADU. Stanford bans it, and Pine Plains' ADU tax break excludes it.

### Gaps
- I did not get the full Pine Plains Town Board minutes for Dec 2025 through Nov 2026, so an STR draft introduced after mid-2026 cannot be ruled out. Call the Town Clerk or Building Department, or check the agendas on pineplains-ny.gov.
- I did not find the outcome of Milan's June 2026 vote.
- I have not verified Ancram's current fee and occupancy terms or Stanford's occupancy and nights caps; the law text was not attached to the application.
- I have not verified the North East and Amenia 2026 status.

## 2. NY State and Dutchess County rules: registry, occupancy tax, sales tax, platform collection

### Takeaway
There is no statewide STR registry. The Dec 2024 law (S885C/A4130C), as amended by a Feb 2025 chapter amendment, lets counties opt in to their own registries. It also imposes state and local sales tax on STRs from Mar 1, 2025, with booking platforms collecting it. Dutchess County's hotel occupancy tax rose from 4% to 5% in 2025. Airbnb collects that tax on its bookings, but the host must collect it on direct or other-platform bookings.

### Cited Findings
- Gov. Hochul signed S885C/A4130C on Dec 21, 2024. The statewide registry was dropped because of cost, and counties may opt in with their own registries. The state still keeps a database fed by quarterly platform reports. A chapter amendment (Ch. 99 of 2025, S.820/A.5686) was signed in Feb 2025, and the law took effect around Apr 21, 2025. — [NYSAC STR page](https://nysac.org/issues/short-term-rentals); [Adirondack Daily Enterprise, Jan 2025](https://adirondackdailyenterprise.com/news/local-news/2025/01/hochul-signs-str-regulation-bill-requests-chapter-amendment); [NYSAC implementation memo May 28, 2025](https://www.nysac.org/media/1aqfhw4f/str-implementation-memo-may-28-2025.pdf)
- Sales tax: "Effective March 1, 2025, New York State and local sales tax is imposed on sales of short-term rental unit occupancy." It applies when the rent exceeds $2 per unit per day. Booking services must register, collect and remit the sales tax and any applicable unit fee on all occupancies they facilitate. Operators have their own registration and filing duties, which cover direct bookings. — [NYS Tax Dept, "Sales tax on short-term rental unit occupancy"](https://tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/publications/sales/short-term-rental.htm)
- The Dutchess Comptroller confirms that sales tax applied to all STR occupancy from Mar 1, 2025. — [Dutchess 2025 Sales Tax Revenue](https://www.dutchessny.gov/Departments/Comptroller/Docs/2025-Sales-Tax-Revenue.pdf)
- Statewide, the first full year of STR sales tax produced about $66M in local sales tax. — [NYSAC 2026 sales tax report, Aug 18, 2026](https://www.nysac.org/media/emqbi21z/2026-sales-tax-report-8-18-2026.pdf)
- Dutchess hotel occupancy tax:
  - The rate rose from 4% to 5% under Dutchess Local Law No. 1 of 2025, effective Jan 14, 2025, with implementation by Mar 1, 2025.
  - STRs must register with the Commissioner of Finance and file quarterly, with returns due 20 days after quarter end.
  - Airbnb has collected and remitted the tax since Mar 1, 2017.
  - Hosts renting outside Airbnb must collect the 5% themselves.
  - The page does not mention VRBO.

  — [Dutchess County Hotel Occupancy Tax](https://www.dutchessny.gov/Departments/Finance/Hotel-Occupancy-Tax.htm); [Dutchess Airbnb FAQ](https://www.dutchessny.gov/Departments/Finance/Docs/Frequently-Asked-Questions-Airbnb.pdf)

### Inferences
- The likely guest-facing tax stack in Dutchess is about 13%: sales tax of about 8.125% plus the 5% occupancy tax.
  - The 8.125% figure is from my general knowledge, not verified in this session: 4% state, 3.75% Dutchess, 0.375% MCTD.
  - Taxes are added on top of the rate and do not reduce host revenue. They do affect price competitiveness.
- The practical compliance steps are:
  - Register for NY sales tax as an operator if taking any direct or Hipcamp bookings.
  - Register with Dutchess Finance for occupancy tax. The county advises this even for Airbnb-only hosts.
  - Confirm whether VRBO and Hipcamp remit Dutchess occupancy tax.

### Gaps
- I could not tell whether Dutchess County has opted in to a county STR registry under the 2025 state law.
- I have not verified that VRBO or Hipcamp collect the Dutchess occupancy tax.
- I have not confirmed the exact combined Dutchess sales tax rate from tax.ny.gov in this session.

## 3. STR market data 2025–2026: Pine Plains neighbors, seasonality, farm-stay premium

### Takeaway
Among the neighbors, Ancram is the strongest comparable, at about $70K a year, $557 ADR and 42% occupancy. Amenia, Millerton and Millbrook are weaker, at roughly 32–33% occupancy. The markets are tiny, with 13–27 listings each, so averages are noisy. Seasonality runs about 2.8 times from January to August.

Farm stays carry experiential appeal, but I found no quantified ADR premium. Airbnb reports the typical US farm-stay host earned about $8K in 2025. Hipcamp claims $8–15K a year for an average active host. Both figures are platform marketing.

### Cited Findings
- **Ancram** (AirROI, Nov 2025 to Oct 2026), 22 active listings:
  - ADR $557, occupancy 42.3%, average revenue $70,362 per listing
  - monthly revenue percentiles: 25th $2,560, median $5,430, 75th $9,530, 90th $19,700
  - best month August (~$13,800, ~2.8 times January); worst month January (~$4,870)
  - occupancy ranges from ~30% in January to 58% in July; ADR peaks at ~$565 in August and bottoms at ~$465 in March and April

  — [AirROI Town of Ancram](https://airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-ancram)
- AirROI's earlier Ancram window (Apr 2025 to Mar 2026) showed $72,445/yr, $540 ADR and 38.4% occupancy. — same source, search snippet
- **Amenia** (AirROI, Apr 2025 to Mar 2026): about $24,129/yr, $304 ADR, 32.8% occupancy, $101 RevPAR. The older 2025 AirROI page showed 12.9% YoY growth. — [AirROI Town of Amenia](https://airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-amenia)
- **Millerton** (Rabbu, Apr 27, 2026): 13 active listings, $225 ADR, 32% occupancy (the state average is 40%). — [Rabbu Millerton](https://rabbu.com/airbnb-data/millerton-ny)
- **Millbrook** (Rabbu, Apr 2026): 13 listings, $442 ADR, 32% occupancy, and +133% YoY growth in listings. — [Rabbu Millbrook](https://rabbu.com/airbnb-data/millbrook-ny)
- BnbCalc's "Taghkanic, Gallatin and Ancram" cluster (Oct 2026): 37 listings, $60.2K average revenue, 12.4% occupancy. This conflicts sharply with AirROI's occupancy, probably because of methodology (calendar vs. available nights). — [BnbCalc via search](https://www.bnbcalc.com/markets/us/livingston-ny)
- **Clinton** (AirDNA): 52% occupancy, $265.6 ADR. This is a regional comparison only; AirDNA's Millerton values were not visible. — [AirDNA](https://www.airdna.co/top/us/new-york/millerton)
- Pine Plains STR supply grew from 22 (2019) to 32 (Q3 2023). — [Herald](https://www.newpineplainsherald.org/pine-plains-opens-the-door-to-short-term-rental-regulations/)
- Farm stays:
  - The typical Airbnb farm-stay host earned about $8,000 in 2025, and farm-stay hosts collectively earned about $120M in 2025.
  - Airbnb launched a "Farm to Stay" grant program.

  — [Airbnb Newsroom, Farm to Stay](https://news.airbnb.com/farmtostay); [Insurance Journal, Feb 10, 2025](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2025/02/10/811419.htm)
- Hipcamp:
  - The "average active host makes $8,000–$15,000 per year." The most active hosts make more than $100K, and the least active a few thousand.
  - Hosts keep 90% (a 10% host fee).
  - The figures are undated marketing.

  — [Hipcamp Help Center](https://support.hipcamp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025121411-How-much-money-can-I-make-as-a-Hipcamp-Host)
- In 2022 the average Hipcamp host in Mendocino County earned $7,500/yr. This is stale. — [BusinessWire 2022](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220606005045/en)

### Inferences
- Pine Plains (~$51K, $483 ADR, 39%) sits between Ancram (~$70K) and Amenia (~$24K).
- A well-presented farmhouse with acreage, views and animal experiences could plausibly reach the 75th-percentile band (Ancram 75th percentile about $9.5K/month in peak), but that is not guaranteed.
- A realistic gross range for a 3–4 BR farmhouse is roughly $40–75K/yr, with high variance. That is an inference from the comps, not a source.
- The $8K Airbnb farm-stay "typical" figure is national and includes rooms and small units. It is not a ceiling for an entire Hudson Valley farmhouse.
- Hipcamp is most relevant as an add-on: tent or RV sites, or Harvest Hosts, on other parts of the acreage. It would add low-five-figure income at best, and town zoning for campgrounds would need checking.

### Gaps

- I did not find Harvest Hosts host-earnings data.
- I did not fetch AirROI pages for Milan, Stanford or North East.
- AirDNA Pine Plains and Millerton numbers were not visible because they are paywalled.

## 4. Operating costs for a Hudson Valley STR

### Takeaway
The main recurring costs are:
- the Airbnb fee: 3% split fee, or the 15.5% host-only fee, which is mandatory for hosts using PMS and channel-manager software since Oct 27, 2025
- cleaning: $50–150+ per turn nationally; a farmhouse will be at the high end
- management: 15–30% full-service, ~10% partial
- dedicated STR insurance

I found no Hudson Valley-specific cost data. All figures are national benchmarks.

### Cited Findings
- Airbnb moved hosts using PMS or channel managers to a single 15.5% host-only fee from Oct 27, 2025. Some sources report extension to all hosts (from Dec 1, 2025 or Oct 2026). The dates are inconsistent and unverified against Airbnb. PriceLabs-type tools suggest marking up the Airbnb channel by about 18–18.5%. — [Uplisting](https://support.uplisting.io/en/article/airbnb-host-service-fee-1tsjyed/); [Hostfully 2026](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/airbnb-host-fees/); [Truvi](https://truvi.com/?p=19958)
- Hipcamp takes 10% from hosts. — [Hipcamp](https://support.hipcamp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025121411-How-much-money-can-I-make-as-a-Hipcamp-Host)
- Management:
  - Full-service management typically costs 15–30% of booking revenue; other sources cite 25–50% or 10–40%.
  - Half-service costs about 10% and excludes cleaning and maintenance.
  - Onboarding runs $500–2,000.
  - Watch for markups on cleaning fees.

  — [Tidy 2026](https://www.tidy.com/airbnb-management-fees); [Hostaway](https://www.hostaway.com/airbnb-management-fees-a-complete-breakdown); [Fairly](https://www.fairly.com/blog/airbnb-management-fees)
- Cleaning runs $50–150 per turnover nationally, more for larger homes. — [RedAwning](https://www.redawning.com/vacation-rentals/pm/post/airbnb-management-fees-a-complete-breakdown-of-all-the-costs)
- Town compliance costs:
  - Stanford: $500 per 2 years plus an inspection.
  - Ancram: $250.
  - Milan's proposed fire-code upgrades were described by a host as potentially "cost prohibitive."

  — [Stanford](https://stanfordny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Short-Term-Rental-Permit-Application-1.pdf); [Herald](https://www.newpineplainsherald.org/milan-weighs-tighter-rules-for-short-term-rentals/)
- Insurance:
  - Standard homeowners policies typically exclude STR business activity, landlord policies are designed for long-term tenants, and home-sharing endorsements are "weak coverage." These claims come from a vendor.
  - VRBO's host protection is liability-only.

  — [Proper Insurance](https://www.proper.insure/blog/do-i-need-short-term-rental-insurance/); [Bankrate](https://www.bankrate.com/insurance/homeowners-insurance/short-term-rental-insurance)

### Inferences
- An illustrative self-managed P&L on $50K gross, built from the benchmarks above (not a sourced figure):

  | Item | Estimate |
  |---|---|
  | Airbnb host-only fee | ~$7.75K |
  | Cleaning (~60–80 turns at $150–200) | ~$10–14K |
  | Utilities, internet, propane or oil, supplies | ~$6–9K |
  | STR insurance | ~$1.5–3K |
  | Maintenance reserve | ~$2–4K |
  | PMS and dynamic pricing | ~$0.5–1K |

- That leaves roughly 30–45% net before mortgage, property tax and depreciation.
- A full-service manager at 20–30% would roughly halve the net.
- A one-time furnishing and setup budget for a 3–4 BR house is not sourced here. Typical rules of thumb are $15–40K, but that is unverified (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- I found no local Hudson Valley management-company fee quotes, for example Red Cottage-affiliated managers, or local cleaner rates.
- I found no sourced furnishing or setup cost for the region.
- I found no STR insurance premium quotes.
- I found no utility cost data for a heated farmhouse.

## 5. Agritourism liability, waivers, combined farm and STR insurance

### Takeaway
NY's 2017 agritourism law gives farms limited liability for the inherent risks of farm activities, provided they post signage, train staff, warn visitors and take reasonable care. It is not blanket immunity, and coverage reports do not mention lodging or farm stays. I found no new agritourism liability law enacted in 2025–2026; related bills were pending. Insurance is the bigger issue. Standard farm and homeowners policies likely exclude STR guests, and I found no NY carrier product confirmed to combine a working farm with an STR.

### Cited Findings
- The 2017 NY law, signed by Gov. Cuomo in Oct 2017, amended the General Obligations Law:
  - It gives limited liability for the inherent risks of agritourism, such as horseback riding, u-pick and tours.
  - Farms must post signage marking public pathways and buildings, train employees, warn visitors of inherent risks, and take reasonable care against foreseeable risks.
  - It is "not blanket immunity."
  - Lodging is not mentioned.

  — [Farm and Dairy, Oct 30, 2017](https://www.farmanddairy.com/?p=453874); [NY Senate, Helming 2017](https://www.nysenate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2017/pamela-helming/senator-helming-announces-signing-bill-support); [Times Union](https://blog.timesunion.com/capitol/archives/278190/new-liability-law-aims-to-protect-new-yorks-agritourism-industry/)
- Pending 2025–26 bills:
  - S1607 would limit liability for u-pick operations.
  - A2320 covers landowner recreational and horseback liability and an "equine activity safety code act"; it is in committee.
  - I found no evidence that either was enacted.

  — [NY Senate S1607 PDF](https://legislation.nysenate.gov/pdf/bills/2025/s1607); [Legilist A2320](https://legilist.com/states/new-york/bill/2025-2026/a-2320)
- An Assemblymember (Woerner) passed legislation in the Assembly aimed at lowering insurance costs for agritourism. Its status is unclear. — [NY Assembly](https://assembly.ny.gov/mem/Carrie-Woerner/story/70704)
- For farm-building holiday lets, brokers recommend an independent broker with both farm and STR expertise. This is UK-based guidance. — [Alan Boswell](https://alanboswell.com/news/thinking-about-diversifying-into-holiday-lets)
- An Airbnb-on-a-horse-farm insurance question on BiggerPockets went unresolved. — [BiggerPockets](https://biggerpockets.com/forums/52/topics/711016-airbnb-on-horse-farm)
- Stanford requires a liability certificate showing the premises is "rated as a short-term rental." Pine Plains could adopt a similar rule. — [Stanford application](https://stanfordny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Short-Term-Rental-Permit-Application-1.pdf)

### Inferences
- A farm-stay operator should:
  - get written confirmation from the farm insurer (Farm Family, NY Farm Bureau Member Benefits / Nationwide, or a farm broker) on whether STR guests and guest-animal interactions are covered, or buy a separate STR policy (Proper or similar) plus farm liability
  - check for gaps between the two policies, especially guest injuries in barns, from animals or from equipment
  - comply with the 2017 law's signage, warning and training conditions
  - use a written guest waiver and acknowledgment of risk covering animal interaction, handled through the Airbnb house rules and a pre-arrival digital waiver
- A waiver's enforceability for negligence under NY law is limited. GOL §5-326 voids waivers at places of recreation where a fee is paid. That statute is from my general knowledge and was not verified this session, so it needs attorney review.

### Gaps
- I did not get the statute text or section number for the 2017 law (likely GOL Article 18-B), and I could not confirm whether overnight stays fall under "agritourism activity."
- I found no Cornell agritourism guidance on farm stays in this session.
- I found no carrier page confirming a combined farm plus STR product in NY, and no premium figures.
- I have not confirmed whether S1607, A2320 or the Woerner bill was enacted in 2025–2026.
