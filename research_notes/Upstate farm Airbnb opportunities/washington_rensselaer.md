## STR regulations and performance (2025–2026); demand drivers

### Takeaway
STR rules are light. No town STR law was found for Cambridge or the Rensselaer towns, and Fort Ann is the only WashCo town with its own registry. Gross per-listing revenue is modest at about $18K–23K/yr with 28–38% occupancy, so a farm-stay STR alone will not cover a $600K+ mortgage. Break-even will need two units, farm income, or both.

### Cited Findings
**Performance (AirROI, trailing 12 months)**
- Town of Cambridge: ADR $216, occupancy 32.5%, avg annual revenue $18,249/listing, 25 active listings. July is the peak month (about $2,720), March the low (about $1,400). October has the highest occupancy (about 42%). Booking lead time is 48 days. Data covers Nov 2025–Oct 2026 — [AirROI Cambridge](https://airroi.com/report/world/united-states/new-york/town-of-cambridge)
- Town of Stephentown: 31 listings, all entire homes. ADR $448, occupancy 27.9%, revenue $19,222 (−7.1% YoY), RevPAR $88. August is the peak (about $4,390, 43% occupancy) and April the low. Data covers Nov 2025–Oct 2026 — [AirROI Stephentown](https://airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-stephentown)
- Town of Greenwich: revenue $21,544, occupancy 37.6%, ADR $218, RevPAR $89. August is the peak and April the low. This is a 2026 page, from a search snippet — [AirROI Greenwich](https://airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-greenwich)

- Hoosick and Grafton: bnbcalc shows "$51.3K" revenue and "15.9%" figures for 9 listings. The methodology differs and the figures are not comparable — [bnbcalc Albany](https://www.bnbcalc.com/markets/us/albany-ny)
- NY State averages for comparison: $36,572 revenue, 40% occupancy, $380 ADR — [AirROI Stephentown](https://airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-stephentown)

**Regulation**
- None of the Cambridge or Stephentown listings display a permit or registration number. AirROI cautions that this does not mean no rules apply — [AirROI Cambridge](https://airroi.com/report/world/united-states/new-york/town-of-cambridge); [AirROI Stephentown](https://airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-stephentown)
- Village of Cambridge zoning (2015) does not define STRs. Use must fit an allowed use or get a special use permit (investor-site claim) — [STR Profit Map](https://www.strprofitmap.com/regulations/NY/cambridge)
- Fort Ann is the only WashCo town with its own STR registry/law — [Glens Falls Chronicle](https://glensfallschronicle.com/?p=59633); [Fort Ann STR law](https://www.townoffortannny.gov/uploads/7/3/6/3/73635113/short_term_rental_law%5B43%5D.pdf)
- Washington County amended its occupancy tax so that all STRs pay 4% — [Glens Falls Chronicle](https://www.glensfallschronicle.com/?p=64567)
- Rensselaer County: one secondary site says the county has not created a registry under the state law and levies a 5% occupancy tax (LL 5 of 2017). Airbnb help pages show 3%. **These conflict** — [cityrulelookup](https://cityrulelookup.com/counties/rensselaer-county-ny/short-term-rentals); [Airbnb help 2319](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2319)
- The NY 2025 state STR law lets counties create registries. Towns keep the power to regulate or ban STRs, and state sales tax applies to STRs regardless — [NYSAC FAQ](https://www.nysac.org/media/dhchq0e0/str-faq-3-28-25.pdf); [NYSAC memo](https://nysac.org/media/moinlpz1/str-implementation-memo-3-25-25.pdf); [NY DOS STR presentation 2026](https://apa.ny.gov/files/planning/forum/2026/Panel%20Presentations/2026%20Zoning%20Presentations/A4-DOS-Short-Term%20Rentals.pdf)

### Inferences
- The two-unit model changes the economics. At about $20K gross per unit, the house plus a studio or guesthouse could gross roughly $35–45K. That covers taxes, insurance and operations, but not a full mortgage on $750K. A large down payment or meaningful farm and agritourism income is needed to break even.
- Stephentown's high ADR reflects the Berkshires spillover (Hancock, Jiminy Peak, Tanglewood). Cambridge, Salem and Greenwich peak in summer and fall from the Saratoga track (July–Labor Day), foliage and the Cambridge arts/food scene (Hubbard Hall, local farms). These demand drivers are general knowledge and were not sourced in this pass.

## Local farm ecosystem

### Takeaway
Washington County is one of NY's most active ag counties. It has two USDA-inspected small slaughter and processing plants close to Cambridge, Salem and Hoosick, which is a real advantage for a small livestock operation.

### Cited Findings
- Washington County had 750 farms in the 2022 Census of Agriculture, down 18% from 2017 — [USDA NASS County Profile](https://nass.usda.gov/Publications/AgCensus/2022/Online_Resources/County_Profiles/New_York/cp36115.pdf)
- Locust Grove Farm / Smokehouse, Argyle: USDA FSIS very small plant doing meat slaughter, meat processing and poultry processing. Family-run since 1973 — [FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/fsis-inspected-establishments/locust-grove-farm)
- Eagle Bridge Custom Meat & Smokehouse, Eagle Bridge (Hoosick/Cambridge line): USDA FSIS slaughter and processing, described as a humane slaughterhouse — [FSIS](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/fsis-inspected-establishments/eagle-bridge-custom-meat-and-smokehouse); [Edible Capital District](https://ediblecapitaldistrict.ediblecommunities.com/eat/humane-slaughterhouse-eagle-bridge-custom-meat)
- Statewide, processing capacity is a bottleneck. About 16% of surveyed NY processors had USDA inspection — [Cornell Small Farms](https://smallfarms.cornell.edu/?p=29330)
- NY FarmLink's Farmland Finder lists conserved farms in Rensselaer County for sale or lease (Fried Farm, Berry Patch at Stone Wall Hill) — [NY Farmland Finder](https://nyfarmlandfinder.org/find-a-farm)

### Inferences
- Having USDA plants within about 20–30 minutes means a small grass-fed beef, lamb or pastured poultry operation can sell retail cuts to STR guests and farm-stand customers. This supports the farm-stay and agritourism angle and can count toward NY ag-exemption income thresholds.

### Gaps
- The current operating status and booking wait times at both plants were not confirmed.
- The Cambridge Valley Farmers Market, Hubbard Hall and Agricultural Stewardship Association (conservation easements) were not sourced in this pass.
