## Q2. STR regulations and STR performance for the towns of the top listings

### Takeaway
In Ulster, rules are tightening. Rochester (Accord, part of Kerhonkson) reportedly set its 2027 cap for new non-owner-occupied permits at zero. Olive's non-owner-occupied cap is full for 2026. Marbletown allows one owner-occupied plus one non-owner-occupied permit per owner. Sullivan towns are generally lighter: Callicoon and Delaware require a permit with no cap, and Cochecton is unregulated but considering registration. Livingston Manor is the one town with town-level STR data: 24 listings, $431 ADR, 36.6% occupancy and about $42.8k average annual revenue (TTM Nov 2025–Oct 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Ulster County (county registration and 4% occupancy tax apply everywhere)**
- Countywide: registration plus a 4% county occupancy tax, with town permits layered on top. — [Offsite / Fresh Air guides via search](https://www.offsite.camp/post/short-term-rental-rules-hudson-valley)
- **Marbletown (Stone Ridge, part of Accord)**: Local Law No. 4 of 2019. STRs are an accessory use in all districts except Industrial, with an annual permit, county registration and a yearly inspection. Occupancy is capped at 10 guests, at 2 per bedroom, with one rental at a time. Each owner may hold **1 owner-occupied plus 1 non-owner-occupied permit**. A 2025 law restricts STRs on properties with an ADU, which matters for a guest-cottage plan. — [Marbletown LL4-2019 (ecode360)](https://ecode360.com/MA1321/laws/LF1230305.pdf); [Haus 2026 guide](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/)
- **Rochester (Accord, part of Kerhonkson)**: §140-26A (Local Law 3 of 2021), a Short-Term Transient Rental permit on a two-year cycle with an annual inspection. There is a yearly cap on new non-owner-occupied permits, while owner-occupied permits are uncapped. **Haus reports the Town Board set the 2027 cap at zero** (updated Nov 2026). This could not be corroborated in Hudson Valley One or town minutes. Another guide says non-owner-occupied STRs are a "Commercial Use" needing Planning Board approval. — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/); [Town LL3-2021](https://townofrochester.ny.gov/files/2021/08/Local-Law-3-2021.Short-Term-Transient-Rental.08_16_2021.ForVoteAug26-1.pdf); [Palisade Stays](https://palisadestays.com/str-rules/rochester)
- **Gardiner**: Haus (Nov 2026) found no dedicated STR law. A proposed 4% occupancy tax and annual registration have unconfirmed status, and Haus found no record that the "100-permit cap" proposal was ever adopted. A 2025 guide had said a law was "pending". — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/); [Fresh Air 2025](https://freshairny.com/blog/2025-guide-hudson-valley-catskills-str-regulations)
- **New Paltz (Town)**: annual registration and inspection for non-owner-occupied rentals (Ch. 110, LL4-2021), with no cap. However, the zoning law does not list STR as a use, so whole-home STR may not be allowed. The Village of New Paltz is stricter. — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/)
- **Olive**: a permit is required (Ch. 114, amended 2025). The non-owner-occupied cap is reset each January and was **full for 2026 with a waiting list**. Owner-occupied permits are uncapped, with one permit per owner. — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/)
- **Saugerties (Town)**: annual permit (LL2-2022, Ch. 194) and yearly inspection. Single-family homes and permitted accessory apartments qualify. No cap and no owner-occupancy rule, though the town reserves the right to add a cap. — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/)
- **Esopus**: annual permit (§123-13, LL8-2021). Non-owner-occupied rentals need Planning Board site plan approval, a host within about 15 driving miles and $1M liability insurance. **Permits do not transfer on sale.** No cap. — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/)
- **Plattekill**: LL4-2025, with 1 owner-occupied plus 1 non-owner-occupied permit per person. The board can impose a cap by unpublished resolution. — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/)
- **Wawarsing (Kerhonkson portion, Ellenville)**: LL2-2024, a two-year permit with annual inspection and a cap on new non-owner-occupied permits; how full it is is unknown. — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/)
- **Woodstock** (for reference): permits are capped townwide with a waiting list. Non-owner-occupied rentals face limits on nights and weekends (historically 180 days or 26 weekends). — [Haus](https://www.str.haus/blog/ulster-county-ny-short-term-rental-regulation-by-town/); [HV1 2019](https://hudsonvalleyone.com/2019/05/18/woodstocks-short-term-rental-regs-set-for-passage)

**Sullivan County**
- **Callicoon**: law adopted 2016. A permit and code-enforcement inspection are required, with no cap on the number of STRs. — [River Reporter](https://www.riverreporter.com/stories/the-blessings-and-blights-of-short-term-rentals,62940); [Fresh Air](https://freshairny.com/blog/2025-guide-hudson-valley-catskills-str-regulations)
- **Delaware (Jeffersonville, Callicoon hamlet area)**: law adopted 2019, with a permit, annual renewal and safety inspection. — [River Reporter](https://www.riverreporter.com/stories/the-blessings-and-blights-of-short-term-rentals,62940); [Fresh Air](https://freshairny.com/blog/2025-guide-hudson-valley-catskills-str-regulations)
- **Bethel**: a draft permit law (stays of 31 days or less) was introduced in 2023. A 2025 commercial guide lists it as regulated, with egress and parking requirements. Adoption is not confirmed. — [River Reporter](https://www.riverreporter.com/stories/bethel-considers-airbnb-law,90170); [Sullivan County Democrat](https://www.scdemocratonline.com/stories/bethels-concerns-over-short-term-rentals,94746)
- **Cochecton**: reported Oct 2026 as unregulated and unregistered, with the town considering a registration rule. — [Citizen Portal](https://citizenportal.ai/articles/11824638/New-York/Sullivan-County/Cochecton/Resident-offers-to-run-local-study-of-short-term-rentals-as-town-considers-registration-rule)
- **Village of Monticello**: banned STRs under 30 days (Feb 2026), which signals a tightening trend in the county. — [Sullivan County Democrat](https://www.scdemocratonline.com/stories/monticello-bars-short-term-rentals,224551)
- Brokers warn that "Upstate NY towns take aim at short term rentals". — [Country House Realty](https://countryhouserealty.com/blog/buyer-beware-towns-take-aim-at-short-term-rentals)
- Not checked: Rockland (Livingston Manor, Roscoe) and Tusten (Narrowsburg) STR rules.

**STR performance**
- **Livingston Manor (AirROI, TTM Nov 2025–Oct 2026)**: 24 active listings, average revenue $42,814, ADR $431, occupancy 36.6%, RevPAR $164. Peak occupancy was 48.7% (Aug 2026) and the low was 25.2% (Feb 2026). Median monthly revenue is about $3,380, the top 25% earn $6,250+ and the top 10% earn $12,300+. Median ADR is about $360 and median occupancy about 30%. The booking window averages 38 days. The small sample makes these figures volatile. — [AirROI Livingston Manor](https://www.airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/livingston-manor)
- **Catskill (Greene County) benchmark, sources conflict**: AirROI reports $25,727 average revenue, 30.6% occupancy and $359 ADR (Aug 2025–Jul 2026). AirROI for the Town of Catskill reports 200 listings, 39% occupancy and $342 ADR. AirDNA reports 333 listings, $50.5k revenue, 47% occupancy and $338 ADR. Rabbu reports 29% occupancy and $281 ADR. — [AirROI](https://www.airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/catskill); [AirROI Town](https://www.airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-catskill); [AirDNA](https://www.airdna.co/vacation-rental-data/app/us/new-york/catskill/overview); [Rabbu](https://rabbu.com/airbnb-data/catskill-ny)
- **National context**: median US entire-home occupancy was 34%, and the median nightly rate rose from $228 to $265 year over year (12 months to Aug 2026). — [AirROI calculator](https://www.airroi.com/calculator)

### Inferences
- A non-owner-occupied Airbnb in the Town of Rochester (Accord) or Olive is likely not permittable for 2027. Buyers there would need owner occupancy, an existing permit (check transferability), or a town without a cap. Marbletown (Stone Ridge) and Saugerties remain open in Ulster.
- If the buyer lives in the main farmhouse, an owner-occupied permit for a guest cottage is generally the easier route in Ulster. Marbletown's 2025 ADU restriction needs checking first.
- Livingston Manor's average of about $43k per year for one well-run unit could plausibly cover a large share of carrying costs (taxes plus insurance plus some debt service) but not a full mortgage on $800k+. "Break even" likely needs a cash-heavy purchase or two rentable units.

### Gaps
- AirROI pages for Kerhonkson, Accord and Callicoon returned 404 (no page). No AirDNA or Rabbu town-level data was retrieved for Stone Ridge, Accord, Callicoon or Jeffersonville.
- The Rochester zero cap for 2027 rests on one secondary source (Haus) and needs confirmation with the Rochester town clerk.
- Rockland and Tusten STR laws were not researched.

## Q3. Recent comparable sales, area advantages and risks

### Takeaway
Only one verified 2025–2026 farm-with-house comp was found: 578 Bethlehem Rd, Callicoon Center, which sold for $760k on 37 acres (about $20.5k per acre all-in), with taxes of about $12.6k per year. Sullivan property taxes are high in dollar terms, about 2.16% effective, and the county's 2026 levy proposal was +9.1%. Area draws include Delaware River tourism, the Catskills and the Gunks, but STR rules are tightening in both counties.

### Cited Findings
- **Comp: 578 Bethlehem Road, Callicoon Center ("Birdland")**: listed at $795,000, **sold at $760,000** (page dated Feb 1, 2026; sale date not stated). 37.01 acres across two lots in two townships. 1908 farmhouse, 6 bd / 1.5 ba, 2,400 sq ft. Swimming pond with dock, apple orchard, former smokehouse shed, attached garage. **Taxes about $12,557 per year.** That works out to about $20,500 per acre with the house, or about 4.4% under list. — [Country House Realty](https://countryhouserealty.com/listings/578-bethlehem-road-callicoon-center-ny)
- **Asking-price benchmarks** (not sales): Accord farms of 17–18 acres with a house ask $950k–$1.35M (about $53k–$78k per acre). Sullivan's 344 Midway Rd asks about $5.4k per acre and 187 Hahn Rd about $8.4k per acre. — sources in Q1
- **Sullivan taxes**: a third-party calculator puts the effective rate at 2.16% (median bill $5,082 on a $234,800 home). It ranks the county mid-pack in NY, 31st lowest of 62, so "highest in NY" is not supported. The 2026 county budget proposal included a 9.12% levy increase (about $114 per $100k assessed) and used $5.5M of fund balance. The final adopted figure is unconfirmed. — [Calcuris](https://calcuris.com/us/property-tax-calculator/new-york/sullivan-county/); [Citizen Portal](https://cpo.soar.com/articles/6835206/Sullivan-County/New-York/Sullivan-County-committee-reviews-tentative-2026-budget-proposes-912-property-tax-increase)
- Sullivan had the steepest sales-tax decline in NY in the first half of 2026, which signals fiscal pressure. — [WJFF Radio Catskill](https://wjffradio.org/sullivan-county-faces-steepest-sales-tax-decline-in-ny-for-first-half-of-2026/)
- STR saturation and community pushback are documented in Sullivan. — [River Reporter "blessings and blights"](https://www.riverreporter.com/stories/the-blessings-and-blights-of-short-term-rentals,62940)

### Inferences
- Budget for about $10k–$15k per year in taxes on a $750k–$950k Sullivan farm. Enrolling in an NY agricultural assessment could reduce the land portion but needs qualifying farm income.
- Advantages: Livingston Manor and Roscoe (fly-fishing, Beaverkill), Callicoon and Narrowsburg (Delaware River), and Accord, Kerhonkson and Stone Ridge (Shawangunks and Minnewaska, about 2 hours from NYC). Risks: the Delaware River and Esopus/Rondout corridors carry flood exposure (check FEMA maps per parcel), seasonal STR income swings by about 2.5x from February to the peak months, and rules keep tightening.
