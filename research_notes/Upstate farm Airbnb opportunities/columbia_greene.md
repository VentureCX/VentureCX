## Q2. STR regulations and performance for top-listing towns

### Takeaway
Columbia County requires county occupancy-tax registration for every STR (<30 days). Airbnb has remitted that tax since April 1, 2026. Town-level permit rules for Ghent, Claverack, Hillsdale, Copake, Chatham, and Austerlitz could not be found online; Germantown has drafted an STR law with a one-rental-per-owner concept. In Greene, the Town and Village of Catskill and the Town of Hunter (capped at 250 permits) require permits; Windham has no STR law. Rural Columbia towns gross roughly $35–46K a year per average Airbnb listing; Hudson and Catskill run $45–52K.

### Cited Findings
**Regulation, Columbia County**
- Anyone renting a building or rooms for under 30 consecutive days must register with the county and pay the occupancy tax. A town or village permit does not replace county registration, and the county number is needed for town permits — [Columbia County Occupancy-STR](https://www.columbiacountyny.gov/611/Occupancy-Short-Term-Rentals); [County FAQ](https://www.columbiacountyny.gov/m/faq?cat=35#question-197)
- Quarterly payments started in 2026. Airbnb collects and remits county occupancy tax from April 1, 2026 (other platforms don't). The county stayed in the state STR registry after the June 2026 opt-out window. The City of Hudson runs its own registration and tax — [County site](https://www.columbiacountyny.gov/611/Occupancy-Short-Term-Rentals); secondary: [bnbcalc Hudson guide](https://www.bnbcalc.com/blog/short-term-rental-regulation/Hudson-New-York-Guide)
- Germantown: draft STR law with hearings in 2023–2024. A commenter objected to a one-rental-per-owner limit, and a Jan 2024 draft allows up to four "seasonal" rentals. Third-party sources say a permit system was adopted, but the law number and date weren't confirmed. A Jan 2026 draft of the full zoning code is pending — [Germantown 2023 hearing](https://germantownny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/2023-07-25-tbpubhearing.pdf); [Jan 2024 draft](https://germantownny.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Short-Term-Rental-Regulation-Draft_012524.pdf); [StrProfitMap](https://www.strprofitmap.com/regulations/NY/germantown); [2026 draft code](https://germantownny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Draft-Germantown-Full-Code-Town-Board-Submission-4-1.21.26-LaBella.pdf)
- The 725 Route 66 listing claims "Airbnb is fully permitted" — [Nest Seekers](https://www.nestseekers.com/5748740/welcome-to-wynt-farmhouse-set-on-nearly-13-pastoral-acres-along-a)

**Regulation, Greene County** (Berardi Realty blog, Sept 3, 2026)
- Town of Catskill: Chapter 133 permit required, adopted April 2022, $500 initial and $200 renewal. A revoked permit bars the owner for one year — [ecode360 Ch. 133](https://ecode360.com/39128216); [Berardi Realty](https://berardirealty.com/blog/greene-countys-short-term-rental-rules-arent-one-law-theyre-three-different-bets)
- Village of Catskill: permit required since Nov 2020, $500 initial and $200 renewal — [Berardi Realty](https://berardirealty.com/blog/greene-countys-short-term-rental-rules-arent-one-law-theyre-three-different-bets)
- Town of Hunter: Local Law 2 of 2023 caps permits at 250, with a $500 fee and $1,000 compliance bond — [Berardi Realty](https://berardirealty.com/blog/greene-countys-short-term-rental-rules-arent-one-law-theyre-three-different-bets)
- Town of Windham: draft law withdrawn after a 1,400+ signature petition. No STR law in effect — [Berardi Realty](https://berardirealty.com/blog/greene-countys-short-term-rental-rules-arent-one-law-theyre-three-different-bets)
- Greene County levies a 4% occupancy tax. The county issued a June 2025 RFP for STR compliance services (secondary source) — [Berardi Realty](https://berardirealty.com/blog/greene-countys-short-term-rental-rules-arent-one-law-theyre-three-different-bets); [StrProfitMap Greene](https://www.strprofitmap.com/regulations/NY/greene)
- No Cairo or Durham STR law was found.

## Q3–Q4. Comparable sales ($/acre) and area advantages and risks

### Takeaway
No clean 2025–2026 $/acre data exists for farms with houses. Asking-price aggregates put Columbia land at a ~$32–34K/acre median (all land types) and farm listings at about $15K/acre. Value is driven by the house, not acreage. Main advantages are Amtrak Hudson access, Hudson and Catskill draw, and rising STR revenue. Main risks are town-by-town permit uncertainty, Germantown-style per-owner limits, and permit caps (Hunter).

### Cited Findings
- Columbia County land listings: average asking $34,074/acre (median $32,586), all land types. Farm-specific median $14,999/acre (4 listings) — [Lands of America](https://landsofamerica.com/Columbia-County-NY/all-land); [Land.com](https://www.land.com/Columbia-County-NY/farms)

- NY statewide farm real estate average was $4,150/acre in 2024 (USDA), far below Hudson Valley levels — [CNYBJ](https://www.cnybj.com/new-york-farmland-values-rise-this-year-usda-says/)

- Greene (Windham, Greenville, Durham) offers more acres per dollar, but Durham-level STR revenue (~$34K) is lower.
