# Delaware County / Schoharie County / Otsego edge (NY): Farm + Airbnb Opportunities (researched 2026-11-17)

Method note: LandWatch, LandSearch, Realmo, Farmflip, Zillow-type pages all returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches. Listing data below therefore comes mostly from search-engine snippets/aggregator summaries. Snippets often lack dates, and several conflict on price, address or status. **Treat all listing statuses as UNVERIFIED unless marked otherwise.** Days on market and taxes were mostly unavailable. Nothing here was confirmed live on the MLS as of 2026-11-17.

## Q1. Active listings under ~$1M (ideally $300K–$800K): house suitable for STR, barn/outbuildings, 20–150 acres open land, bonus second dwelling

### Takeaway
Few listings in this region match the full profile (house + barn + 20–150 open acres + second dwelling, under $1M) in the inventory I could see. LandWatch showed only about 8–15 Delaware County farm/ranch listings and about 6 in Schoharie. The best leads are (1) a 174.91-acre operating farm in Bovina Center at about $550K and (2) a 36.6-acre 1850s farmhouse and barn near Cooperstown at $649K. Most other leads are land with no house, stale, pending, or over budget. None of the sub-$1M leads I found has a confirmed second dwelling.

## Q2. STR regulations and STR performance by town

### Takeaway
Regulation is light to moderate and runs on registration rather than bans.
- Andes and Margaretville already have STR registration/permit laws.
- Roxbury's law was still in draft as of July 2026.
- The Town of Middletown has a law pending, with a hearing on December 2, 2026.
- Otsego County requires an occupancy-tax Certificate of Authority, and NY State sales tax has applied to STRs since March 2025.
- Village of Cooperstown has a STR law, but its current rules were not confirmed.

On AirROI's data, typical listing revenue is modest at about $25K–$38K a year with roughly 30–37% occupancy. A single Airbnb alone will not carry a mortgage on a $550K–$650K property.

### Cited Findings: regulations
- **Roxbury.**
  - The Town Board proposed an STR law with a registration system modeled on **Andes and Margaretville**: fire/safety compliance documents, an evacuation plan and a property description, and the town can block operators who don't register.
  - The County Planning Board recommended approval on July 1, 2026, with concerns about inspection staffing and enforcement.
  - Estimates of Roxbury STRs range from 100 to 400.
  - Source: [The Reporter, July 5, 2026](https://www.the-reporter.net/stories/county-planning-board-urges-caution-as-roxbury-weighs-short-term-rental-law,255557)
- **Roxbury hearing.** A hearing was held July 13, 2026. Dozens of residents urged caution, the board did not vote, and a further hearing is planned. — [CitizenPortal](https://citizenportal.ai/articles/9510407/New-York/Delaware-County/Roxbury/Roxbury-hears-broad-public-concern-at-Short-Term-Rental-hearing-board-takes-no-vote)
- **Andes.**
  - Requires a Transient Rental Permit.
  - One third-party guide also claims a local manager within 30 miles, notice to neighbors within 200 ft, and an **owner-present-overnight rule that would effectively bar whole-home rentals**. This comes only from the guide and is **unverified**. It is a critical item to check with the Andes town clerk.
  - Source: [STR Profit Map, Andes](https://www.strprofitmap.com/regulations/NY/andes); [FreshAir NY 2025 guide](https://freshairny.com/blog/2025-guide-hudson-valley-catskills-str-regulations)
- **Town of Middletown (Margaretville area).** Public hearing on STR Local Law #3 set for **December 2, 2026, 6:00 PM**. — [Town of Middletown](https://www.townofmiddletowndelcony.gov/)
- **Delaware County occupancy tax.** Reported at **2%, effective March 2025**; source is a third-party guide. — [FreshAir NY](https://freshairny.com/blog/2025-guide-hudson-valley-catskills-str-regulations)
- **Otsego County.**
  - Hosts need a Certificate of Authority to collect occupancy tax under **Local Law 2 of 2025**, and must file quarterly returns.
  - Source: [Airbnb Help, article 4180](https://www.airbnb.ca/help/article/4180)
  - Otsego occupancy tax is cited as 6%, and NYS sales tax has applied to STR stays since March 1, 2025. — [BNBCalc Oneonta guide](https://www.bnbcalc.com/blog/short-term-rental-regulation/Oneonta-New-York-Guide)
- **Village of Cooperstown.** After a moratorium, the village adopted a "controversial" STR law. A letter mentions about 25 single-family homes holding STR permits in the village. — [AllOtsego](https://www.allotsego.com/cooperstown-adopts-new-short-term-rental-law/); [AllOtsego letter](https://www.allotsego.com/northrup-short-term-rentals-should-be-limited)
  - The Town of Hartwick (where 269 W Hill Rd sits) is outside the village. No Hartwick STR law was found.
- **Schoharie County and towns (Sharon Springs, Middleburgh, etc.).** No STR law found.

## Q4. Advantages and risks

### Takeaway
The advantages are very cheap acreage (about $2,000–$3,300/acre for big tracts), established Catskills STR demand in Roxbury, Andes and Bovina, and Cooperstown's high summer ADRs. The risks are low year-round occupancy (about 30–37%), heavy summer seasonality, growing town STR registration laws, and NYC DEP watershed review for septic and building work near streams in the Delaware County watershed towns.

### Cited Findings
- **Seasonality.**
  - Harpersfield peak months average 46% occupancy against about 32% for the year. — [AirROI Harpersfield](https://airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-harpersfield)
  - Town of Delaware: August is the strongest month and April the weakest. — [AirROI Town of Delaware](https://airroi.com/airbnb-data/united-states/new-york/town-of-delaware)
- **Demand trend:** Andes RevPAR rose 16.2% year over year to August 2026. — [AirDNA](https://www.airdna.co/vacation-rental-data/app/us/new-york/andes/supply)
- **NYC DEP watershed rules.**
  - DEP reviews and approves new or repaired septic systems (SSTS) and soil disturbance in the watershed.
  - Its regulated activities include impervious surfaces such as paved driveways next to a stream, and houses or structures next to a stream or watercourse.
  - Stormwater pollution prevention plans (SWPPPs) are required for certain construction within **100 ft of a watercourse or wetland**.
  - Sources: [NYC DEP Watershed Regulations](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dep/environment/regulations.page); [DEP SWPPP applicant guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dep/downloads/pdf/watershed-protection/regulations/applicants-guide-to-swppp.pdf); [DEP SSTS applicant guide](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dep/downloads/pdf/watershed-protection/regulations/applicants-guide-to-sssc.pdf)
  - These rules matter for adding a cottage or converting a barn in Bovina, Andes, Roxbury, Delhi, Middletown, Stamford and Kortright. Schoharie and Otsego are outside the NYC watershed, though Gilboa sits on the Schoharie Reservoir.
- **Tax and registration load:**
  - Delaware County occupancy tax 2% — [FreshAir](https://freshairny.com/blog/2025-guide-hudson-valley-catskills-str-regulations)
  - Otsego County occupancy tax plus a Certificate of Authority — [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.ca/help/article/4180)
  - NYS sales tax on STRs since March 2025 — [BNBCalc](https://www.bnbcalc.com/blog/short-term-rental-regulation/Oneonta-New-York-Guide)
- **Property-tax example:** the Schoharie two-dwelling farm (19.6 acres) paid about $11,942 a year in combined taxes. — [Country House Realty](https://countryhouserealty.com/listings/877-state-route-443-schoharie-ny)
- **Housing politics:** the County Planning Board raised housing affordability concerns about STRs in Roxbury. — [The Reporter](https://www.the-reporter.net/stories/county-planning-board-urges-caution-as-roxbury-weighs-short-term-rental-law,255557)

### Inferences
- **Delaware County watershed towns (Bovina, Andes, Roxbury):** the strongest Catskills brand, but expect more red tape for a second dwelling.
- **Otsego near Cooperstown:** concentrated baseball-season demand (roughly June–August youth tournaments) and no NYC DEP watershed review.
- **Schoharie:** the cheapest and least regulated, but has the weakest STR data.
- **Agricultural assessment:** a small farm can lower property taxes through NY agricultural assessment, for example by leasing hayfields on a 150-acre parcel. This was not researched here.

### Gaps
- Internet and cell coverage for the specific listing addresses was not researched. Check the NYS broadband map and the FCC map for each address.
- Drive times were not verified.
- Local news on the farm-to-table scene (Bovina, Andes, Delhi, Cobleskill) was not gathered.
- Winter occupancy figures for specific towns were not available, beyond the April low noted above.
