# Financing and Conservation Easement / PDR Options for Pine Plains, NY Farmland

Research date: 2026-11-17. Figures are flagged where they are stale or from secondary sources.

## 1. Current (late 2026) land and farm loan rates and terms

### Takeaway
Borrowing is expensive in late 2026. The conventional 30-year mortgage averages about 7.1% (Freddie Mac, Nov 12, 2026). The FSA direct farm ownership rate is 6.25% (Oct 2026, official), and FSA's down payment loan, at about 2.25%, is the cheapest money available, but only to borrowers who qualify as farmers. Farm Credit East does not publish rates. Its 1.25% patronage rebate lowers the effective rate on its loans by roughly a point. Raw land loans from banks or aggregators typically run 6.5–10% with 25–35%+ down.

### Cited Findings
**Conventional mortgage benchmark (Freddie Mac PMMS)**
- Nov 12, 2026: 30-year fixed 7.11%, 15-year fixed 6.45%. The prior week (Nov 5) was 7.18% / 6.57%. A year earlier the rates were 6.24% / 5.49%. — [Freddie Mac PMMS](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)
- Rates peaked near 7.4% in early October 2026, the highest in nearly three years. The 30-year rate was 7.32% on Oct 22, 7.24% on Oct 29 and 7.18% on Nov 5. — [WCAX/AP, Nov 5, 2026](https://www.wcax.com/2026/11/05/average-us-30-year-mortgage-rate-falls-718-this-week-its-third-straight-decline/)
- Q4 2026 forecasts were about 7.30% (MBA) to 7.40% (Fannie Mae). This comes from a secondary aggregator. — [The Mortgage Reports](https://themortgagereports.com/17574/mortgage-interest-rate-predictions-forecasts-va-fha-usda-conventional)

**Farm Credit East**

A variable rate can be converted to fixed, which may carry fees. Loans can be fully or partially amortized, with monthly, quarterly or semiannual payments. **Farm Credit East publishes no rates or down payment figures.** — [Farm Credit East Land Loans](https://farmcrediteast.com/FINANCING/Land-Loans)
- Country Living loans include a Country Land Program for rural home lots and vacant land and a Country Home Program for primary residences, including homes with acreage and barns. Terms run 5–30 years. — [Farm Credit East blog, Apr 15, 2025](https://www.farmcrediteast.com/resources/todays-harvest-Blog/250415CountryLivingLoansOffersMoreThanFarmFinancing); [Rural Home Loans](https://www.farmcrediteast.com/FINANCING/Rural-Home-Loans)

**Typical bank and raw land loan terms**
- 2026 land loan rates are 6.5–10% at most lenders. Down payments of 25–35% are typical, and around 35% is common for raw land. These figures come from aggregators and are not primary sources. — [Farmonaut land purchase loan guide](https://farmonaut.com/?p=129217); [Mossy Oak Properties](https://www.mossyoakproperties.com/how-finance-land-best-loan-options-tips/); [FCS America Land Loans 101](https://www.fcsamerica.com/resources/learning-center/land-loans-101)

### Inferences
- Model base case: Farm Credit East land loan at about 7.0–7.75% fixed, which is roughly mortgage-level, with 20–30% down and 20–30 year amortization. Treat patronage as about a 1.0–1.25% rebate on interest paid or loan volume, giving an effective rate of about 6–6.5%. All of this is my estimate and should be confirmed with a quote from the Millbrook/Dutchess branch.
- FSA can blend money. In a Down Payment loan structure the buyer puts 5% down, FSA lends 45% at about 2.25%, and a commercial lender provides 50%. A Joint Financing loan is 4.25% on the FSA share. Either one sharply lowers the blended rate, but only for a qualifying farmer with 3 years of management experience. An investor with no farm operator cannot use FSA directly. A tenant farmer could.
- Pure raw land with no house or ag income should be modeled at 25–50% down and about 8–10% from a local bank.

## 2. Conservation easement / PDR programs and recent deals

### Takeaway
Selling development rights is the realistic way to recover capital in northern Dutchess. Recent Pine Plains-area deals paid about $7,100–7,250 per acre: Jordan Lane Farm, 153 acres for $1,090,063 (Dec 2020), and Chaseholm Farm, 175 acres at an estimated $1,268,770. A 2026 southern Dutchess deal (Obercreek) implies about $13,500/acre. Funding is stacked from state FPIG grants (Round 20 awarded $48.5M in Oct 2026; awards can now reach $3M, and $15M+ remains on a rolling basis), county PMG funds (up to a 50% match), land trust funds and sometimes NRCS ACEP-ALE. These deals generally require an active farm with good soils and a land trust sponsor, and they take years.

### Cited Findings
**Recent Pine Plains and nearby deals**
- **Jordan Lane Farm, Pine Plains:** 153 acres. DEC and Dutchess Land Conservancy announced the easement on Dec 15, 2020. Purchase price $1,090,063, funded by a $982,290 NYS Water Quality Improvement Project grant plus Dutchess County Partnership for Manageable Growth money. DLC holds the easement. The farm helps form a contiguous protected block along Routes 82/83. — [NYSDEC bulletin, Dec 15, 2020](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/NYSDEC/bulletins/2b12ec7)
- **Schultz Hill Farm, Pine Plains:** The Osofsky family of Ronnybrook Dairy protected 185 acres, the "Home Farm," through a purchase of development rights. DLC handled planning and implementation with partner Scenic Hudson, and funding came from NYS Ag & Markets (EPF), Scenic Hudson and DLC. The Lakeville Journal reported it on Jul 7, 2016; this deal is stale. **No dollar amount was published.** — [Lakeville Journal, Jul 7, 2016](https://lakevillejournal.com/local-dairy-protects-185-acres-farmland-critical-regional-foodshed)
- A separate Ronnybrook-related project protected 210 acres in Ancram (Columbia County) with Scenic Hudson and Columbia Land Conservancy, announced May 2017. — [Scenic Hudson press release](https://www.scenichudson.org/press-release/partners-conserve-local-icon-ronnybrook-farm-dairy/)
- **Chaseholm Farm (Pine Plains):** 175 acres, estimated easement cost $1,268,770. Partners DLC, Scenic Hudson and Hudson Highlands Land Trust provided 60%. The date of the county release was not confirmed. — [Dutchess County Executive release](https://www.dutchessny.gov/Departments/County-Executive/34100.htm)
- **Pulver Farm, Pine Plains:** The county closed on a 171.6-acre easement at the base of Stissing Mountain, about 2003 (stale). DLC co-holds it. — [Dutchess County release](https://www.dutchessny.gov/Departments/County-Executive/CE20030930pulverfarm.htm)
- **Obercreek Farm, Town of Wappinger (2026, southern Dutchess):** 143 acres. Funding was PMG $481,981 (25%), NYS Ag & Markets $1,442,378 and DLC $6,000, about $1.93M total. This deal took PMG past 5,000 acres. — [Millerton News](https://millertonnews.com/partnership-for-manageable-growth-hits-5000-acre-preservation-milestone)
- DLC reports it has worked with 443 landowners to protect more than 46,670 acres since 1985, and more than half of its easements are on farmland. — summarized from DLC materials via search; see [DLC Protected Land map, Apr 23, 2026](https://www.dutchessland.org/images/content/Protected_Land_Dutchess_8x11_4-23-2026.pdf)

**NYS Farmland Protection Implementation Grants (FPIG)**
- Round 20 was announced Oct 20, 2026: more than $48.5M to 43 farms, protecting 16,005 acres. More than $15M is still available on a rolling basis. The maximum award was raised to $3M. Projects meeting climate resilience or source-water goals get a one-time incentive payment of 10% of easement value. Easements are perpetual. The Dec 2025 offering was a record $67M, boosted by the Environmental Bond Act. Senator Hinchey's statement names the Columbia and Dutchess Land Conservancies, Dog Wood Farm and Conway Farm among the awardees. Project-level detail is in a linked PDF that was not retrieved. — [Governor's press release, Oct 20, 2026](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-announces-nearly-50-million-preserve-new-york-farmland)
- Earlier rounds: $55M+ for 2,100 acres (Jan 15, 2024); nearly $33M for 12,300+ acres (Jun 6, 2024). — [Governor, Jan 15, 2024](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-announces-more-55-million-awarded-protect-2100-acres-new-york-farmland); [Governor, Jun 6, 2024](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-announces-nearly-33-million-awarded-new-york-farms-protect-more-12300-acres)

### Inferences
- Per-acre easement values (my arithmetic): Jordan Lane is $1,090,063 / 153 = about **$7,125/acre** (2020). Chaseholm is $1,268,770 / 175 = about **$7,250/acre**. Obercreek is about $1.93M / 143 = about **$13,500/acre** (2026, southern Dutchess with higher development pressure). For Pine Plains in 2026 a reasonable range is about **$6,000–10,000/acre**, set by appraisal as before-value minus after-value. It often comes to about 40–60% of fee value. That ratio is an estimate, not sourced.
- The easement payment goes to the landowner of record. A buyer who acquires unprotected land and then sells an easement could recover a substantial share of the purchase price. That requires winning a competitive grant, which favors working farms with prime soils, contiguity with protected land and source-water benefits. The Pine Plains Route 82/83 corridor and Stissing area already have a cluster of protected land, which helps scoring.
- Timeline is typically 2–4+ years from application to closing. That is general knowledge, consistent with multi-year FPIG rounds; there is no local source. Model the easement as a later-year cash inflow, not as day-one financing.
- A bargain sale combines both benefits: the landowner sells below appraised value and deducts the difference.

## 3. Federal and NY State tax benefits of donating an easement

### Takeaway
Under IRC 170(b)(1)(E), a donated qualified conservation easement is deductible up to 50% of AGI, or 100% for qualified farmers (more than 50% of gross income from farming), with a 15-year carryforward. No 2026 change was found. The NY State Conservation Easement Tax Credit is 25% of school, county and town property taxes on the eased land, capped at $5,000 per year. No change for 2026 was found.

### Cited Findings
- Qualified farmers and ranchers can deduct up to 100% of AGI. They must earn more than 50% of gross income from farming, and the easement must keep the land available for agricultural production. Other donors are limited to 50% of AGI. Excess deductions carry forward 15 years. — [NC Dept. of Agriculture federal tax benefits summary](https://www.ncagr.gov/divisions/farmland-preservation/landowners/federal-tax-benefits/download); [GiftLawPro note](https://mylegacy.amazingfacts.org/giftlawpro/glawpro_notices.jsp?ID=8); statute 26 U.S.C. §170(b)(1)(E), §170(h)
- In a bargain sale, income from the sale portion does not count toward the 50% farm-income test. — [NC Dept. of Agriculture](https://www.ncagr.gov/divisions/farmland-preservation/landowners/federal-tax-benefits/download)
- 2026 guides show no change to these rules. — [Uncle Kam 2026 guide](https://unclekam.com/tax-strategy-blog/conservation-easement-tax-deduction-2026-guide/) (secondary)
- The NY credit equals 25% of school district, county and town real property taxes paid on land subject to a conservation easement held by a public or private conservation agency, with a maximum of $5,000 per tax year. — [NYS DTF conservation easement credit](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/credits/conservation_easement_credit.htm); [NYS DEC](https://dec.ny.gov/nature/open-space/conservation-easement-tax-credit); [CT-242 instructions](https://www.nystax.gov/pdf/current_forms/ct/ct242i.pdf)
- The credit is not available to NY S corporations or their shareholders, according to the CT-242 instructions. — [CT-242 instructions](https://www.nystax.gov/pdf/current_forms/ct/ct242i.pdf)
- Do not confuse the easement credit with the farm food-donation credit, which rises in 2026 to 50% with a $20,000 cap. — per search summary of [NY legislation](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/A5660)
- The Tax Court continues to disallow easement deductions that fail technical requirements. Syndicated deals are an IRS listed transaction, and SECURE 2.0 (2022) limits pass-through deductions to 2.5x basis. The disallowance point is from [CPA Practice Advisor](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/?p=176552); the SECURE 2.0 limit is my general knowledge and was not fetched.

### Inferences
- An investor who is not a farmer gets only the 50%-of-AGI limit, so a large donation may take years to use even with the 15-year carryforward. The cash value of a donation is roughly easement value times the marginal federal and NY rate, about 35–45% of easement value at high incomes, which is less than a PDR sale pays. Under the 2.5x-basis rule, a partnership or LLC should hold the land for 3+ years or keep the deduction within limits.
- The NY credit is small, at most $5,000/yr. Example: $20k/yr of school, county and town taxes on eased land gives a $5,000 credit.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 IRS or NY DTF notice confirming the rules were unchanged. They are presumed unchanged.
- The SECURE 2.0 pass-through limit was not verified from a fetched primary source this session.
- Whether the NY credit is refundable for farmers was not verified.
