# Pine Plains-area farm/acreage listings, sold comps, and $/acre benchmarks (research date 2026-11-17)

Method note: The major portals (LandWatch, Land and Farm, LandSearch, Homes.com, Redfin) returned HTTP 403/405 to direct fetches. Listing data below therefore comes mostly from search-engine snippets of those portals, plus pages that could be fetched directly: the eXp Realty page, the Village Green Realty page, Millerton News articles and the NASS report. **Active-listing status could not be confirmed directly for most listings.** Treat every "active" row as "seen on a portal, status unverified" until the MLS confirms it. The most reliable data is the sold comps from Millerton News, which come from Dutchess County Real Property transfer records and are fetched and dated. All $/acre figures are my own arithmetic (price ÷ acres) unless stated otherwise.

## 4. Market trends: northern Dutchess / Hudson Valley land, 2024–2026

### Takeaway
USDA NASS (Jul 31, 2026) puts **New York farm real estate at $4,500/acre in 2026, up 4.7% from 2025.** NY cropland is $4,150/acre (+3.5%) and pasture $2,040/acre (+4.6%). The statewide averages are far below Hudson Valley lifestyle-land prices of about $8K–$30K/acre. Nominal appreciation has run about 3–5%/yr statewide. Locally, the northern Dutchess home market went flat to soft in 2026: fewer sales, many price cuts, and medians flat in Pine Plains and down in the northeast county cluster in Q1.

### Inferences
- Statewide NASS figures (about $4.5K/acre) describe working farmland upstate and are not good comps for Pine Plains. Local raw-land sales of $8K–$21K/acre carry a 2–5x lifestyle/exurban premium over the NY average, and they sit above even the Northeast average of $7,510/acre.
- For a break-even hold, the NASS trend of about 3–5% nominal per year statewide is the most defensible long-run appreciation assumption. Local land values look flat-to-soft in 2026, as shown by Hicks Hill selling 6% under ask, Carpenter Hill (if verified) selling 15% under ask and below its 2021 price, and widespread price cuts. Carrying costs are material: Hicks Hill taxes are about $7.3K/yr, or about 1.4% of the price. Ag assessment (leasing to a farmer) would matter for break-even.

- NY cash rent figures for 2025/2026 were not in the land values report. They are in the separate NASS Cash Rents release, which I did not fetch.
- No AcreValue data was retrieved.
- I did not find the Aug 2025 NASS report itself. The 2025 values above come from the 2026 report's revised table.
