**Prepared:** September 2026 · Companion to `medtech-mro-market-map.md`

## 1. The NJ View

**New Jersey is a large, wealthy, hospital-dense market that is almost entirely in-house on HTM — and its supply side is fragmented into micro-caps.** Two facts frame everything:

- **Demand:** Only **two confirmed outsourced HTM accounts in the entire state** — AtlantiCare → Sodexo (effective June 21, 2025, HTM bundled inside a full facilities package) and Saint Peter's → Crothall (currency unconfirmed). The big five systems (Hackensack Meridian, RWJBarnabas, Atlantic, Virtua, Cooper) are in-house, financially strong (HMH AA-, RWJBH Aa3, Cooper upgraded to A+), and in HMH's case have embedded clinical engineering inside IT. **The classic "large, in-house, financially pressured" lift-out screen returns no clean NJ target.**
- **Supply:** ~20 confirmed NJ-headquartered service companies, mostly $1–5M founder/family-owned shops; **no NJ independent of $20M+ scale exists** — the largest NJ assets were already bought (Major Medical → MTA '24, Biomed Technologies → ISS Solutions '21, Sigma Imaging → InterMed '20, Nationwide Imaging → MXR '18).

**So the NJ play is:** anchor on 2–3 proprietary founder deals (below), sell *supplemental modality coverage* — not lift-outs — into the big five, chase the one displacement (Saint Peter's) and the one distressed re-tender (Hudson Regional), and use the **outpatient imaging channel** (RadNet's NJ Imaging Network across 10 counties, Atlantic Medical Imaging's 29 sites, SJRA, ImageCare) as the fast-contracting beachhead nobody screens for. Labor arbitrage will not sell here — RWJBH's in-house BMET II band ($28–31/hr) already sits at or below TRIMEDX's contractor band — so the pitch is parts, uptime, imaging capability, and capital deferral (NJ's still-active CON regime adds friction to equipment replacement, which favors life extension).

## 4. Who's Already Hunting NJ (competition for deals and contracts)

- **Scientific Safety Alliance (SSA)** — founder/employee-owned roll-up, 15 acquisitions, bought STAT Biomedical (covers NJ); **explicitly markets "a better alternative to PE" to sellers**. Expect them in every founder conversation — build a retention/rollover story before first contact.
- **Crothall HTS** (Wayne, PA) — dominant corridor strategic; holds Saint Peter's; runs an imaging-engineer training program.
- **MTA, InterMed, MXR, Transcat, ISS Solutions (Geisinger)** — all already own NJ footprints from prior acquisitions.
- **Agiliti** — service centers in Oakland, NJ and Mount Laurel, NJ (covers the state from both ends); verify whether these do biomed depot work or only rental logistics — it changes the build-vs-buy calculus.
- **Steris IMS** — repair lab in Hillsborough, NJ (Somerset County). An NJ instrument platform competes with Steris on home turf.
- **TRIMEDX** — no NJ anchor account found (only a Newark "Channel Services" role). **NJ looks like white space for TRIMEDX** — both an opportunity and a warning.

## 5. Ecosystem — Cheap, High-Leverage Moves

1. **Sponsor PAMIA now** (Philadelphia Area Medical Instrumentation Association — active, monthly 2nd-Monday meetings, explicitly covers South/Central NJ; vendor-hosted meetings are a standing format). Lowest-cost credible entry into the regional HTM community.
2. **Revive NJBES** (NJ Biomedical Engineering Society — founded 2006, ~50-person North/Central NJ email list, dormant for lack of volunteers, no website). Owning the North Jersey association vacuum costs almost nothing; no competitor fills it. ("Garden State Biomedical Society" does not exist.)
3. **County College of Morris BMET program** (Randolph — the only true NJ community-college BMET program, ABET-accredited) — establish a co-op slot early; Atlantic Health already owns that relationship. Pair with Thomas Edison State's online A.S./B.S. in Biomedical Electronics as a technician upskilling/retention tool.
4. **Wage benchmarks for modeling:** NJ average BMET ~$35/hr; RWJBH BMET II $28.27–31.21/hr; TRIMEDX Newark $27–33/hr; ~90 open BMET roles statewide (mid-2026).

## 6. Verification Flags Before Outreach

1. **Crothall's Saint Peter's contract status and renewal date** — the single most decision-relevant unknown; evidence is a 2018–22 blog anecdote + undated job req. Verify by phone.
2. **Inspira's HTM posture** — nothing found either way; top primary-research item.
3. **Revenue/headcount undisclosed** for nearly every Tier 1–2 company (only Total Scope $9.8M and Proton ~$1.5M have usable figures) — all sizing is directional.
4. **AtlantiCare/Sodexo economics undisclosed** — the circulating "$70M / 26 sites / 400 employees" figures belong to Sodexo's *Adventist Health* EVS deal (Jan 2026), not AtlantiCare. Keep them out of any model.
5. **Agiliti's NJ sites** — depot capability vs. rental logistics unconfirmed.
6. Valley, Prime NJ, Hudson Regional HTM postures unverified; Salem Medical Center has essentially no current public information.
7. NJ hospital M&A fails unusually often (three collapsed deals in ~18 months, heavy FTC scrutiny) — don't build a pipeline that depends on consolidation-driven re-tenders.
